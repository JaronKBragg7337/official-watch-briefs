### 1. Iranian Drone Attacks on Bahrain/Kuwait and US Self-Defense Strike on Qeshm Island Missile Site
**Label:** Confirmed by Multiple Official Sources  
**Time posted:** November 17–18, 2026 (CENTCOM post Nov 17 ~11:43 UTC)  
**Source accounts:** @CENTCOM (official), US military officials, Bahraini/Kuwaiti defense statements, IRGC claims  
**Links:** https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2122656792358289747  
**Country:** United States / Iran / Bahrain / Kuwait  
**One-paragraph summary:** CENTCOM officially stated that multiple IRGC one-way attack drones launched toward Bahrain were intercepted with no damage to U.S. equipment or injury to American personnel, directly refuting Iranian claims of a successful hit on a U.S. radar system. In response, U.S. fighter aircraft used precision munitions to strike an IRGC missile launch site on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz in a self-defense operation. Iran subsequently claimed additional drone attacks targeting U.S. assets at Ali Al Salem Air Base in Kuwait (including alleged Patriot systems) and further sites in Bahrain; Bahraini and Kuwaiti forces reported intercepting the attacks with no significant damage confirmed by U.S. sources. No U.S. personnel were injured in any of these attempted attacks this week.  
**Estimated confidence:** High

### 3. US-Russia-China Trilateral Summit and Joint Declaration on Nuclear Risks
**Label:** Official Statement (Confirmed by Multiple Official Sources)  
**Time posted:** November 19, 2026 (White House posts throughout the day; declaration released same day)  
**Source accounts:** @WhiteHouse, Chinese MFA/Xinhua, Kremlin  
**Links:** https://x.com/WhiteHouse/status/2123420042520121391 ; https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2026/11/u-s-russia-china-heads-of-state-declaration-toward-peace/ ; https://en.people.cn/n3/2026/1119/c90000-20521189.html  
**Country:** United States / China / Russia  
**One-paragraph summary:** Presidents Trump, Xi, and Putin held a historic first trilateral meeting in Shenzhen on the sidelines of APEC. They issued a four-point “Declaration toward Peace” committing to UN reform so the organization better fulfills its peace and security mandate; increasing production and supply of refined petroleum products with emphasis on stable energy flows; affirming the paramount importance of state consent and immunities of state officials while stating that the ICC and similar bodies must not abuse their jurisdiction; and reaffirming that “a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought,” with a pledge to conduct dialogue on reducing nuclear risks and preventing nuclear proliferation. The declaration does not specifically reference Iran, Ukraine, or other active conflicts.  
**Estimated confidence:** High
