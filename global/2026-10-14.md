### 2. NATO Steadfast Noon Annual Nuclear Deterrence Exercise
**Label:** Military Statement  
**Time posted:** Exercise began 13 October 2026 (announced earlier; running through ~23 October). Official NATO release 8 October.  
**Source account(s):** NATO official site / @NATO  
**Link:** https://www.nato.int/en/news-and-events/articles/news/2026/10/08/nato-holds-annual-nuclear-exercise-steadfast-noon  
**Country:** NATO (hosted in Italy; 17 nations participating)  
**Summary:** NATO launched its long-planned annual Steadfast Noon nuclear deterrence exercise on 13 October in Italy. It involves aircraft and personnel from 17 Allied nations and is described as routine training to maintain readiness and test procedures contributing to the Alliance’s deterrence and defence. No live weapons are used. NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte stated that the Alliance is defensive, the exercise ensures a safe, secure, effective, and credible capability to preserve peace, prevent coercion and deter aggression, and sends a clear message of unity and resolve. Officials emphasized it is not linked to current world events and is not directed against any country. This occurs against a backdrop of prior Russian nuclear rhetoric (including regarding Kaliningrad), which NATO has publicly characterized as unhelpful and not representing an imminent threat to Alliance territory.  
**Estimated confidence:** High
