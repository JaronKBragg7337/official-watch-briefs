## 1. CENTCOM: Strait of Hormuz open; shipping partners briefed; IRGC closure claim rejected

- **Label:** Military Statement
- **Time posted:** Denial 7 October 2026, 5:59 p.m. EDT (21:59 UTC); shipping brief 8 October 2026, 10:53 a.m. EDT (14:53 UTC)
- **Source account:** U.S. Central Command (@CENTCOM)
- **Link:** https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2108209079864766918
- **Country:** United States (Strait of Hormuz; regarding Iran)
- **Summary:** On 8 October CENTCOM said it briefed international shipping partners in a virtual discussion on the Strait of Hormuz. Admiral Brad Cooper, the CENTCOM commander, thanked industry leaders and other U.S. government agencies and said commercial traffic flow is increasing, while acknowledging sacrifices by civilian crews from what the command called unwarranted Iranian attacks. On 7 October the command rejected an IRGC general’s media claim that the strait was closed and under Iranian control, stating traffic was flowing, including about 20 million barrels of crude oil, and that the United States and regional partners control the strait. These posts are status and denial statements, not a new strike or closure order.
- **Estimated confidence:** High

Related denial post: https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2107954036750119346
