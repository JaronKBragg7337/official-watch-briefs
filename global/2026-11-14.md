### 3. FAA Nationwide Ground Stop – Official Determination of No Cyber Attack Evidence
- **Label:** Official Statement
- **Time posted:** 2026-11-13 evening through 2026-11-14 (White House / DHS briefings)
- **Source account:** @DHSgov (Secretary Mullin statement); White House briefings
- **Link:** https://x.com/DHSgov/status/2121422993855291539 (and related White House statements)
- **Country:** United States
- **Summary:** The FAA issued a rare nationwide ground stop lasting more than four hours on 13 November due to instability in air traffic control radar and communications systems attributed to overloaded aging routers/switches. Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin stated there was “zero evidence that this was a cyber attack.” Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy described technical failure of legacy hardware. Airspace reopened ~20:15 ET. Officials emphasized safety of airborne aircraft and ruled out cyber or other malicious causation based on available evidence.
- **Estimated confidence:** High (multiple official statements)
