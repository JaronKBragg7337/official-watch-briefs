- **Time:** November 15–16, 2026
- **Source accounts:** IRGC statements via Iranian state media; US military responses reported by multiple outlets; State Department / US Embassy warnings
- **Links:** Reporting in The National, Fox News, CBS, Al Jazeera, etc.

These incidents occurred concurrent with the arrival of the third U.S. carrier.

### 5. U.S. Intelligence Assessment of Iranian Missile Inventory
- **Label:** Government Claim (U.S. officials)
- **Time reported:** November 14–16, 2026
- **Source:** Wall Street Journal and other outlets citing U.S. officials
- **Country:** United States / Iran
- **Summary:** U.S. officials told the Wall Street Journal that the intelligence community estimates Iran retains between 1,500 and 2,500 missiles in its arsenal. This is described as a low-confidence assessment due to the difficulty of tracking exact numbers of launch-ready or stored missiles. The estimate is presented as countering earlier characterizations of Iran’s missile capabilities as fully decimated. Separately, Iran has publicly announced the lifting of its self-imposed ~2,000 km ballistic missile range limit.
- **Estimated confidence:** Moderate (officials speaking to press; explicitly noted as low-confidence by sources)
