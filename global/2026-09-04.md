- **Source accounts / channels:** Iranian Army statement via Iranian state television and @PressTV (state network); Kuwait Armed Forces General Staff and Kuwait Ministry of Foreign Affairs (as reported in official-aligned Kuwaiti coverage of army/MFA X statements).
- **Links:**
  - https://x.com/PressTV/status/2095429918574121144

  - https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2095595527337623679 (U.S. blockade update same day; no matching CENTCOM confirmation of the Kuwait/UAE base strikes in reviewed posts)
- **Country:** Iran; Kuwait; United Arab Emirates; United States (targeted facilities claimed)
- **Summary:** The Iranian Army said it conducted the 31st phase of “Operation Thunder,” firing missiles and drones at U.S. satellite-communications systems, equipment depots, and fighter-aircraft hangars at Ahmad al-Jaber Air Base in Kuwait and at U.S. force locations and radar systems at Al Minhad Air Base in the UAE. CENTCOM’s contemporaneous public posts focused on maritime blockade statistics rather than confirming or denying this specific wave against Gulf bases.
