## 1. IDF: Hezbollah weapons and rocket launchers concealed in schools in southern Lebanon

- **Label:** Military Statement
- **Time posted:** 2026-09-09 15:35 UTC (thread continued ~15:53–15:56 UTC)
- **Source account:** @IDF (Israel Defense Forces)
- **Link:** https://x.com/IDF/status/2097710462510969048
- **Country:** Israel
- **Summary:** On the international day to protect education from attack, the IDF posted that Hezbollah has repeatedly used schools in southern Lebanon as cover for weapons storage and rocket fire. Specific examples: more than 130 weapons in a school in the Bint Jbeil area; hundreds of weapons including anti-tank rockets and grenades plus UNHCR markings in Al-Khiam; military equipment, uniforms, and Radwan Force certificates in a school in southern Lebanon; and a launcher concealed inside a school in Tir Zibneh from which rockets were fired toward northern Israel. Follow-on posts showed photos and video of recovered weapons and the Tir Zibneh launcher site.
- **Estimated confidence:** High (primary military account documenting its own finds)
