## 1. Secretary Rubio: Iran will not be allowed a nuclear weapon; talks stalled

- **Label:** Official Statement
- **Time posted:** 7 October 2026, morning EDT (joint press conference in Athens; RFE/RL timestamp 7:21 a.m. EDT / 13:21 CET)
- **Source account:** U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio, at the sixth U.S.–Greece Strategic Dialogue with Greek Foreign Minister Giorgos Gerapetritis (reported by CNBC and RFE/RL from the press conference)
- **Link:** https://www.rferl.org/a/rubio-athens-iran-nuclear-weapon-greece/33872014.html
- **Country:** United States (regarding Iran)
- **Summary:** In Athens, Secretary Rubio said Iran will never be allowed to acquire a nuclear weapon and that preventing that outcome is a red line for President Trump: “Iran cannot have a nuclear weapon. Simple, period, end of story.” He said Tehran had failed to take advantage of multiple opportunities to reach an agreement on its nuclear program and declined to specify what level of uranium enrichment, if any, Washington would accept, saying any deal must ensure Iran never has a nuclear weapon. He also said Tehran was trying to build a conventional arsenal large enough to complicate action against its nuclear program. This is a diplomatic red-line statement during a stalled effort to end the seven-month U.S.-led conflict with Iran, not a launch or facility-strike announcement.
- **Estimated confidence:** High that the remarks were made at the Athens press conference
