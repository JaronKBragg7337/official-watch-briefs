- **Label**: Military Statement

- **Link**: https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2126467565253779814

### 2. CENTCOM Disables Commercial Vessel Attempting to Run Iran Naval Blockade
- **Label**: Military Statement  
- **Time posted**: 27 Nov 2026, 16:55 GMT  
- **Source account**: @CENTCOM  
- **Link**: https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2126359352869065046  
- **Country**: United States / Iran  
- **Summary**: CENTCOM reported that on 26 November it disabled the Palau-flagged bulk carrier M/V Pacific Archer in the Gulf of Oman after the vessel departed Bandar Abbas and attempted to run the U.S. naval blockade despite multiple warnings. A U.S. military aircraft struck the ship’s stern with precision munitions, disabling propulsion; the civilian crew was unharmed. The command continues to enforce the blockade, having redirected 158 complying commercial vessels and disabled non-compliant ones (this being among recent such actions).  
- **Estimated confidence**: High (primary official U.S. military source). Note: Some independent tracking reports have questioned the precise port of origin and circumstances; the above reflects the official statement only.
