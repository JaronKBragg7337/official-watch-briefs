### 1. Iran Supreme Leader Orders Geographically Unrestricted Response to Threats

- **Label:** Official Statement
- **Time posted:** Approximately 13:45 UTC, October 31, 2026
- **Source account:** Khamenei Media (@Khamenei_m)
- **Link:** https://x.com/Khamenei_m/status/2116526871030690121
- **Country:** Iran
- **Summary:** Iran's Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei directed senior commanders of the Armed Forces that any level or type of threat must be met with an immediate, impactful, and geographically unrestricted response. The directive, issued during a reported meeting with top military officials, also ordered updates to the military target list to bring all tangible and intangible US and Israeli assets within range of Iranian forces. Iranian state media and multiple outlets confirmed the statement, which comes amid ongoing tensions in the 2026 Iran-US-Israel conflict, active Qatari mediation efforts, and speculation of possible renewed hostilities after US midterm elections. No new attacks were reported as of this brief.
- **Estimated confidence:** High

### 2. US CENTCOM Debunks IRGC Claim of Destroying Sea Drone in Strait of Hormuz

- **Label:** Military Statement
- **Time posted:** 17:55 GMT, October 30, 2026
- **Source account:** U.S. Central Command (@CENTCOM)
- **Link:** https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2116227440347394114
- **Country:** United States
- **Summary:** CENTCOM issued a direct rebuttal to claims by Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) that it had destroyed a U.S. military sea drone (unmanned surface vessel) in the Strait of Hormuz. The command stated the claim is false, consistent with other IRGC statements that week, and confirmed that all U.S. military unmanned surface vessels in the Middle East are accounted for. This includes a squadron of advanced sea drones on patrol in the Strait of Hormuz supporting CENTCOM's defensive layer. The statement occurs against the backdrop of ongoing U.S. naval blockade enforcement, mine clearance operations, and commercial vessel escorts in the region.
- **Estimated confidence:** High
