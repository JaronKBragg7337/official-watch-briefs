### 2. IAEA Expresses Urgent Proliferation Concerns Over Iran’s Nuclear Program
**Label:** Official Statement (IAEA Report)  
**Time Posted:** Report circulated November 13, 2026  
**Source Account:** International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA)  
**Link:** https://www.reuters.com/world/china/iaea-increasingly-concerned-about-iranian-enrichment-plant-its-report-says-2026-11-13/ (reporting on confidential report)  
**Country:** Iran / United Nations  
**Summary:** In a confidential report to member states, the IAEA stated it is increasingly concerned about the Isfahan Fuel Enrichment Plant (IFEP), which Iran declared in 2025 but has not allowed inspectors to access. The Agency does not know the plant’s exact location, status, or whether it contains nuclear material. Due to lack of access since the June 2025 strikes, the IAEA cannot conclude there has been no diversion of declared nuclear material from peaceful activities. It urged Iran to take incremental steps to restore inspections, describing the lack of information as a matter of serious proliferation concern requiring utmost urgency. Iran has cited ongoing security threats from the US as the reason it cannot resume full monitoring.  
**Estimated Confidence:** High (multiple independent reports of the official IAEA report)
