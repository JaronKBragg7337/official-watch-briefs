### 1. Iran – Official Statement / Government Claim
- **Time posted**: ~11–12 November 2026 (X post reported 11–12 Nov; IRNA coverage 12 Nov)
- **Source account**: Mohsen Rezaei (Secretary, Supreme National Security Council) via X / Iranian state media
- **Link**: https://en.irna.ir/news/86319655/Rezaei-US-bases-from-West-Asia-to-Europe-legitimate-targets (IRNA); multiple outlets confirm the X post text
- **Country**: Iran
- **Summary**: Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council Mohsen Rezaei stated that if the United States launches another war against Iran, every military base involved in the aggression—from West Asia to Europe—will be a legitimate target for Iran’s lethal strikes. He added “NO SAFE DISTANCE. NO SAFE HAVEN. NO IMMUNITY.” Related remarks (including to Al Mayadeen) indicated Iran would target distant bases, islands previously considered out of range, and any European base from which U.S. aircraft launched. This follows recent Iranian statements on lifting self-imposed ballistic missile range limits (~2,000 km) and the public unveiling of underground “missile cities” containing ballistic and cruise missiles.
- **Estimated confidence**: High (direct attribution by Iranian state media IRNA and consistent multi-outlet reporting of the post)

### 2. United States – Military Statement
- **Time posted**: 11 November 2026, 18:05 GMT
- **Source account**: @CENTCOM
- **Link**: https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2120578655655014848
- **Country**: United States
- **Summary**: U.S. Central Command stated that U.S. forces continue working around the clock to keep the Strait of Hormuz open for commercial shipping while strictly enforcing the “ironclad naval blockade” against Iran. - **Estimated confidence**: High (official CENTCOM account)
