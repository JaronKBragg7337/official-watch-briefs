**1. IRGC Claims Downing of 54th U.S. MQ-9 Drone over Strait of Hormuz**  
- **Label**: Military Statement / Government Claim  
- **Time posted**: Early 20 October 2026 (IRNA English post ~05:30 UTC; statement timed to early hours local)  
- **Source account**: Islamic Republic News Agency (IRNA English) @IrnaEnglish (reporting IRGC statement); also carried by IRIB and other official outlets  
- **Link**: https://x.com/IrnaEnglish/status/2112416064550175207 (and https://en.irna.ir/news/86297740/)  
- **Country**: Iran  
- **Summary**: The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) Aerospace Force announced that its air defenses, operating under Iran’s integrated air-defense network, intercepted and destroyed a U.S. MQ-9 Reaper drone over the Strait of Hormuz in the early hours of 20 October. No contemporaneous U.S. confirmation or denial has been issued; prior similar IRGC claims have been contested by CENTCOM.

**2. IRGC Claims Mines Halted Two Tankers in Southern Strait of Hormuz**  
- **Label**: Military Statement / Government Claim  
- **Time posted**: 20 October 2026 (~13:50 UTC IRNA English post)  
- **Source account**: IRNA English @IrnaEnglish (reporting IRGC statement)  
- **Link**: https://x.com/IrnaEnglish/status/2112542038218391605  
- **Country**: Iran  
- **Summary**: IRGC public relations stated that two non-compliant tankers attempting to transit a prohibited southern route in the Strait of Hormuz struck naval mines previously laid by IRGC Navy forces, caught fire, and were halted. The statement warned that further use of the mined route would result in additional damage and that continued “mischief” by the U.S. would impose a higher price on oil companies. This follows earlier CENTCOM statements (mid-October) denying Iranian control of the strait and rejecting previous mine-related claims as false.  
- **Estimated confidence**: Moderate
