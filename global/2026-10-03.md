## 1. North Korea ballistic missile launch — ROK JCS, Japan MoD, trilateral call

- **Label:** Confirmed by Multiple Official Sources
- **Time posted:** Detection about 03 Oct 2026, 06:30 KST (02 Oct 2026, 21:30 UTC). Japan Ministry of Defense alert posted 02 Oct 2026, 21:42 UTC. Japan MOFA trilateral call posted 03 Oct 2026, 09:03 UTC. ROK presidential office emergency meeting announced later on 3 October.
- **Source account:** Republic of Korea Joint Chiefs of Staff (statement carried by Reuters, Yonhap/Korea Herald, and Chosun Biz); Japan Ministry of Defense / Self-Defense Forces (@ModJapan_en); Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan (@MofaJapan_en); ROK Office of National Security (Blue House notice).
- **Link:** https://x.com/ModJapan_en/status/2106137770750345234 ; https://x.com/MofaJapan_en/status/2106309134173999272 ; https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/north-korea-fires-unidentified-projectile-toward-sea-off-east-coast-seoul-says-2026-10-02/
- **Country:** North Korea (detected by South Korea, Japan, and the United States)
- **Summary:** South Korea’s Joint Chiefs of Staff said it detected one ballistic missile launched from the Wonsan area toward the East Sea (Sea of Japan) at about 6:30 a.m. local time on 3 October, flying more than 700 km. Seoul and Washington said they were still analyzing the exact type; reporting noted the range is consistent with a short-range ballistic missile, while authorities left open a reduced-range medium- or intermediate-range possibility. Japan’s Ministry of Defense posted that an object that may be a ballistic missile had been launched from North Korea and that updates would follow; Japan’s Coast Guard, citing the defense ministry, said the object appeared to have come down and warned ships. Japan’s MOFA said Deputy Director-General Ariyoshi held a Japan–U.S.–ROK foreign-ministry telephone meeting with ROK Director-General Baek Yong-jin and U.S. Deputy Assistant Secretary David Wilezol. The ROK Office of National Security convened an emergency security meeting chaired by National Security Director Wi Sung-lac. The launch followed a Friday statement by Kim Yo-jong rejecting talks with Seoul and an apology demand over a DMZ mine blast that wounded three South Korean soldiers. No North Korean state confirmation of this specific launch was identified in this scan.
- **Estimated confidence:** High (ROK JCS detection statement, Japanese defense alert, Japanese foreign-ministry post, and Reuters/Yonhap reporting of the same event)

- **Time posted:** 02 Oct 2026 (IRNA English timestamp 5:32 p.m.; Mehr News Agency timestamp 7:29 p.m., Tehran local)
- **Source account:** Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps statement, carried by IRNA and Mehr News Agency (Iranian state media). Not an independent confirmation of capability or intent.

- **Country:** Iran (directed at the United States and Israel)
- **Summary:** Marking the second anniversary of Operation True Promise II (Iran’s October 2024 ballistic-missile attack on Israel), the IRGC said the era of cost-free threats is over and that any aggression would draw a painful response. IRNA quoted the statement as saying the IRGC, claiming intelligence dominance over U.S. and Israeli assets, is ready to give a decisive, immediate, painful, and even deadlier response than True Promise II to any threat, aggression, or miscalculation. The statement also commemorated Ayatollah Ali Khamenei and commanders it said were killed in the recent wars, and claimed U.S. and Israeli operations forced Washington to seek a ceasefire. This is a rhetorical military warning on an anniversary, not a launch notice, a transfer announcement, or a confirmed new test.
- **Estimated confidence:** High that the statement was issued through Iranian state media; Low on any implied operational timeline, which was not specified

## 4. UKMTO: crude tanker struck by unknown projectile east of Oman

- **Label:** Official Statement
- **Time posted:** UKMTO warning reported early 03 Oct 2026 (Reuters 02 Oct 2026, 23:31 UTC). A separate UKMTO product (Warning 148-26) was logged earlier on 02 Oct at 11:22 UTC for a Hormuz-outbound tanker strike.
- **Source account:** United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO), reported by Reuters. UKMTO is the UK government’s maritime security reporting center, not a U.S. combatant command.
- **Link:** https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/crude-oil-tanker-struck-by-unknown-projectile-off-oman-ukmto-says-2026-10-02/
- **Country:** United Kingdom reporting (incident off Oman; Strait of Hormuz context; no attacker named)
- **Summary:** UKMTO said the master of a crude-oil tanker reported being struck by an unknown projectile about four nautical miles east of Oman. All crew were reported safe and no environmental impact was reported at the time. A shipping-security summary of UKMTO products said an earlier Friday warning (148-26, 11:22 UTC) described an outbound Hormuz tanker struck by an unknown projectile, with a small fire and blackout that were brought under control and the ship remaining underway, with no casualties. Neither product named the ship, flag, projectile type, or attacker. This is a maritime-security alert, not an attribution to Iran or the Houthis.
- **Estimated confidence:** High that UKMTO issued the warnings (Reuters citing UKMTO); Low on responsibility, which was not stated
