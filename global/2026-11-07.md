### 1. CENTCOM Establishes Task Force Iron Gate to Disrupt Iranian Ground Supply Routes

- **Label:** Military Statement  
- **Time Posted:** November 7, 2026 (approximately 13:30 GMT / 8:30 AM EST)  
- **Source Account:** U.S. Central Command (@CENTCOM) and official CENTCOM website  
- **Link:** [https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2119059918120526182](https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2119059918120526182)  
  Official release: [https://www.centcom.mil/MEDIA/PUBLIC-RELEASES/Article/4644250/centcom-launches-task-force-to-disrupt-iranian-ground-transit-routes-supporting/](https://www.centcom.mil/MEDIA/PUBLIC-RELEASES/Article/4644250/centcom-launches-task-force-to-disrupt-iranian-ground-transit-routes-supporting/)  
- **Country:** United States / Iran  
- **Summary:** On November 7, 2026, U.S. Central Command Commander Admiral Brad Cooper directed the establishment of Task Force Iron Gate, an internal staff of approximately 20 civilian and military personnel based in Tampa, Florida. The task force will coordinate with other U.S. government agencies to prevent the movement of advanced weapons systems, dual-use technologies, sanctioned materials, and illegal revenue streams across Iran’s land borders and the Caspian Sea. This initiative reinforces the ongoing U.S. naval blockade, which has prevented Iran from exporting more than $25 billion in crude oil in recent months, with strict enforcement continuing against vessels entering or departing Iranian ports. The goal is to further constrain Iran’s ability to finance terrorism, rebuild weapons factories, and replenish its military capabilities.  
- **Estimated Confidence:** High
