### 1. North Korea: Short-range ballistic missile salvo  
**Label:** Confirmed by Multiple Official Sources (ROK Joint Chiefs of Staff + Japan Ministry of Defense)  
**Time posted / reported:** ~12:30 p.m. local (0330 GMT), 29 October 2026  
**Source accounts / authorities:** Republic of Korea Joint Chiefs of Staff (JCS); Japan Ministry of Defense; South Korean presidential National Security Office  
**Links:** Multiple official statements reported via Yonhap, Jiji Press, Reuters, Al Jazeera (primary ROK JCS and Japan MOD releases)  
**Country:** North Korea (DPRK) / South Korea / Japan / United States (allied coordination)  
**One-paragraph summary:** North Korea fired approximately 10 short-range ballistic missiles from the Wonsan area on its east coast toward the East Sea (Sea of Japan). The missiles flew roughly 250 km. South Korea’s JCS detected the launches, strengthened surveillance, and is sharing data with the United States and Japan while maintaining readiness for further activity. Japan’s Defense Ministry confirmed a possible ballistic missile launch that did not enter Japanese territory or its exclusive economic zone; no damage to ships or aircraft was reported. South Korea’s National Security Office called the launches a serious violation of UN Security Council resolutions and urged an immediate halt. The salvo occurred amid ongoing border tensions (land-mine incident and propaganda-loudspeaker threats) and follows earlier October launches.  
**Estimated confidence:** High  

### 2. Russia: Successful Bulava SLBM test launch from Pacific Fleet SSBN  
**Label:** Official Statement / Military Statement  
**Time posted / reported:** 28 October 2026 (announced by Russian Ministry of Defense / Pacific Fleet press service)  
**Source accounts / authorities:** Russian Ministry of Defense; Pacific Fleet  
**Links:** Official MoD/Pacific Fleet announcement (carried by Izvestia, RT, Defence Blog, Russianforces.org, and others citing the primary statement)  
**Country:** Russia  
**One-paragraph summary:** The Russian Pacific Fleet’s Project 955A Borei-A-class strategic ballistic missile submarine *Generalissimo Suvorov* conducted a successful training launch of a Bulava intercontinental ballistic missile (nuclear-capable SLBM) from a submerged position in the Sea of Okhotsk. Warheads reached the designated Chizha test range in the Arkhangelsk region on schedule. The launch was part of planned combat training to maintain the readiness of the sea-based nuclear deterrent. Video and confirmation were released by the Defense Ministry.  
**Estimated confidence:** High  

### 3. United States: CENTCOM official statements on Strait of Hormuz  
**Label:** Official Statement / Military Statement  
**Time posted:** 27–28 October 2026 (X posts)  
**Source accounts:** @CENTCOM (U.S. Central Command)  
**Links:** https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2115523900071116849 and https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2115070611550581236  
**Country:** United States / Iran  
**One-paragraph summary:** CENTCOM publicly rejected Iranian/IRGC claims that commercial vessels have struck sea mines in the Strait of Hormuz and that U.S. forces have abandoned the waterway. CENTCOM stated that all internationally recognized primary transit routes have been cleared of sea mines by U.S. forces and remain clear, with thousands of commercial vessels continuing to transit safely. U.S. forces remain present and vigilant in the Strait and surrounding waters; the CENTCOM commander recently overflew the area. The statements emphasize that the United States and regional partners, not Iran, control the international waterway.  
**Estimated confidence:** High
