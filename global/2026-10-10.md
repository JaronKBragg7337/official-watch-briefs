## 1. Iranian Army Ground Forces: over 50 combat and support units deployed along borders

- **Label:** Military Statement / Government Claim
- **Time posted:** Statements circulating 10 October 2026 (reports from Mehr News, Fars News, Press TV equivalents; interviews with commander)
- **Source account:** Iranian Army Ground Forces Commander Brigadier General Ali Jahanshahi (via state media: Mehr News Agency, Fars, etc.)
- **Link:** https://en.mehrnews.com/news/248460/Over-50-combat-support-units-deployed-along-Iran-s-borders (and parallel state media reports)
- **Country:** Iran
- **Summary:** Brigadier General Ali Jahanshahi, commander of the Iranian Army Ground Forces, stated that more than 50 combat and support units have been deployed along the country’s borders, extending from the northwest to the southwest, across eastern regions, and along the Makran coast. Units include special forces, rapid reaction, special operations, mechanized, armored, artillery, engineering, missile, and drone formations. He described the deployments as ensuring security and readiness to confront threats, drawing on lessons from prior conflicts, and noted reconnaissance of potential hostile entry points (including abandoned airports). This is presented by Iranian state media as ongoing preparedness amid regional tensions.
- **Estimated confidence:** High that the commander made these statements via official Iranian media; Moderate on exact force posture details (official claim, not independently verified by external sources in this scan)

## 2. Iran nuclear chief: enrichment inseparable; will not abandon or hand over uranium

- **Label:** Government Claim / Official Statement
- **Time posted:** 8–9 October 2026 (state media and international reports of remarks)
- **Source account:** Mohammad Eslami, head of the Atomic Energy Organization of Iran (via Iranian state media)
- **Link:** https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1550207/iran-will-not-abandon-uranium-enrichment-or-hand-over-its-uranium-nuclear-chief-says.html (Reuters-sourced) and parallel Mehr/ABNA reports
- **Country:** Iran
- **Summary:** Mohammad Eslami stated that uranium enrichment is “an inseparable part” of the nuclear process and that Iran will neither abandon enrichment nor hand over its uranium stockpile. He dismissed renewed U.S. demands for meaningful reductions in enrichment capacity as “nothing new.” The remarks come amid ongoing diplomatic exchanges aimed at ending the conflict and reopening the Strait of Hormuz. U.S. officials have reiterated that Iran must never obtain a nuclear weapon.
- **Estimated confidence:** High that these are Eslami’s stated positions as reported by multiple outlets citing Iranian state media

## 3. Independent reporting: Iran retains missile and drone production capacity; White House disputes

- **Label:** Independent Reporting (Western officials via Bloomberg); Government Claim (White House response)
- **Time posted:** Reporting published 10 October 2026
- **Source account:** Bloomberg citing anonymous Western officials; White House spokesperson Anna Kelly
- **Link:** https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-911221 and https://www.ynetnews.com/article/sy7u2hwogl
- **Country:** Iran / United States / Russia (alleged supplier)
- **Summary:** Western intelligence assessments cited by Bloomberg indicate Iran has preserved key weapons factories and retains the ability to manufacture ballistic missiles and drones, remaining “well-stocked” with ballistic and certain anti-ship weapons and able to replenish stocks relatively quickly. One official claimed Russia has been supplying Iran with missiles. White House spokesperson Anna Kelly rejected the assessment, stating that President Trump “obliterated Iran’s nuclear facilities during Operation Midnight Hammer” and that Operation Epic Fury destroyed the conventional shield (ballistic missiles, production facilities, and navy). No official Iranian confirmation of Russian missile transfers appeared in the scanned posts.
- **Estimated confidence:** Moderate on the existence of the intelligence assessments (multiple Western media reports); Low–Moderate on specific Russian transfer claims (anonymous sources, denied or unaddressed by Russian MoD in reporting); High that the White House publicly disputed the findings
