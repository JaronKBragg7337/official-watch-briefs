## 2. U.S. Defensive Strikes on Iran’s Qeshm Island

**Label:** Government Claim / Multiple Independent Reports  
**Time:** Reported late November 2 – early November 3, 2026 (on the eve of U.S. midterm elections)  
**Source:** Multiple anonymous U.S. officials speaking to major outlets (Wall Street Journal, CNN, Fox News, CBS, Jerusalem Post, etc.); Iranian state media (IRNA) reported explosions  
**Links:**  
- https://www.timesofisrael.com/us-carries-out-defensive-strikes-on-irans-qeshm-island-in-strait-of-hormuz/  
- https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-913644  
- https://www.stripes.com/theaters/middle_east/2026-11-03/us-strikes-iran-qeshm-island-hormuz-23348317.html  
**Country:** United States / Iran  

**Summary:** U.S. officials confirmed to multiple news organizations that U.S. forces conducted limited, defensive strikes on Iranian targets on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz. The action followed the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) firing a surface-to-air missile toward a U.S. aircraft operating over the strait. Officials described the strikes as targeting the launcher and as not constituting a resumption of major combat operations or the start of an expanded campaign. Iranian state media reported several explosions on the island. The Pentagon referred comment requests to CENTCOM, which did not issue a public statement on this specific incident at the time of reporting. This occurs amid an ongoing U.S. naval blockade of Iranian ports in the Strait of Hormuz, continued low-level exchanges, and public speculation about possible larger U.S. operations after the November 3 midterm elections. President Trump has publicly referenced remaining nuclear-related targets such as Pickaxe Mountain.

**Estimated confidence:** Moderate (consistent accounts from multiple U.S. officials to independent outlets; no direct public statement from CENTCOM or the White House on the specific strikes)
