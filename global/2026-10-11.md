- **Source account**: U.S. Central Command (@CENTCOM)
- **Link**: https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2109073217176637447
- **Country**: United States (action in Gulf of Oman, related to Iran blockade)
- **One-paragraph summary**: U.S. Central Command stated that its forces disabled the Panama-flagged commercial cargo vessel M/V Ocean Molica (also known as Arika Sun) in the Gulf of Oman on October 10 after the crew ignored repeated warnings and attempted to run the ongoing naval blockade against ships entering or departing Iranian ports. CENTCOM reported that since the blockade resumed on July 14, four commercial vessels have been disabled, 135 ships turned around, and 10 tankers linked to the IRGC shadow network destroyed. Mariners were advised to heed blockade warnings, monitor Notice to Mariners, and contact U.S. naval forces on channel 16 in the Gulf of Oman and Strait of Hormuz approaches.
