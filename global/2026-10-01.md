- **Source account:** Iranian government spokesperson Fatemeh Mohajerani, via state-run IRNA; also remarks attributed to President Masoud Pezeshkian. Not a direct foreign-ministry X post in this scan.
- **Link:** https://www.news18.com/agency-feeds/iran-indicates-it-received-official-response-to-latest-ceasefire-offer-10361817.html
- **Country:** Iran (counterparty: United States)
- **Summary:** Iranian officials indicated they had received an official U.S. response to Tehran’s latest proposal to end the war. Spokesperson Fatemeh Mohajerani told IRNA that Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi presented that response to President Masoud Pezeshkian at a cabinet meeting. Pezeshkian was quoted saying Iran would try to bring an agreement to fruition on a “win-win” basis and would stand up for Iranian rights. Officials did not disclose the contents of the U.S. response or whether it was a rejection. The same briefing covered Araghchi’s UN General Assembly trip and Iran’s conditions, conveyed through mediators, for reopening the Strait of Hormuz, including a reported offer to reopen the strait in a week if the United States lifted a blockade of Iranian ports, released frozen assets, and waived oil sanctions. President Trump had publicly rejected that package days earlier. This item is a government claim carried by state media and wire reporting, not a published U.S. official text of a reply.
- **Estimated confidence:** Moderate (Iranian official spokesperson is on the record via IRNA/AP; substance of the U.S. reply is undisclosed and unconfirmed by a U.S. official account in this window)
