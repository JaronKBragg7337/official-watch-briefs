## 1. United States / CENTCOM: IRGC Ballistic Missiles Fired at Two U.S. Navy Warships; CENTCOM Strikes Three Iranian Crude Oil Carriers

- **Label:** Military Statement
- **Time posted:** Saturday, September 5, 2026 13:38:53 GMT (statement); follow-on commander video Saturday, September 5, 2026 14:43:37 GMT
- **Source account:** @CENTCOM
- **Links:**
  - https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2096231596449767452
  - https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2096247889366290840
- **Country:** United States; Iran
- **Summary:** U.S. Central Command said CENTCOM forces struck three Iranian crude oil carriers on September 5 after the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps launched ballistic missiles toward two U.S. Navy warships patrolling regional waters. CENTCOM stated that a U.S. aircraft carrier and a guided-missile destroyer successfully evaded multiple unprovoked Iranian attacks and that no American personnel were harmed. After the failed attacks, CENTCOM said it permanently disabled the IRGC crude oil carriers M/T Downy off Kharg Island and M/T Stark 1 near Jask, and completely destroyed the unladen crude oil carrier M/T Kylo (also known as “Noxen”) in the Gulf of Oman after directing the crew to abandon ship. CENTCOM described the three tankers as part of a multibillion-dollar shadow network funding the IRGC and its regional proxies and said Iran has no means to defend them. Adm. Brad Cooper, CENTCOM commander, said: “If you shoot at two of our ships, we will impose an even higher economic cost — taking out three of yours. We will not hesitate to defend American forces, and if necessary, destroy Iran’s limited and exposed oil fleet.” This is an official U.S. military announcement of both an incoming ballistic-missile attack on U.S. warships and a retaliatory strike on Iranian oil tankers. It is not a DEFCON announcement, declaration of war, or ceasefire.
- **Estimated confidence:** High
