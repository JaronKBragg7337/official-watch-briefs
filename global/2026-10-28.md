### 1. U.S. State Department / Embassy Security Alert & Ordered Departure – Saudi Arabia
**Label:** Official Statement
**Time posted:** Alert dated 28 October 2026 (effective order 23 October 2026); Embassy Riyadh post ~04:41 UTC 27 October
**Source account:** U.S. Embassy Riyadh (@USAinKSA) and official embassy site (sa.usembassy.gov)
**Link:** https://sa.usembassy.gov/security-alert-u-s-mission-to-saudi-arabia-october-28-2026/
**Country:** United States (affecting Saudi Arabia; Iran-supported Houthis)
**Summary:** The Department of State ordered non-emergency U.S. government employees and family members to leave Saudi Arabia effective 23 October due to safety risks from Iranian-supported Houthi hostilities, including missile and UAV/drone attacks on civilian airports and critical infrastructure that caused casualties and travel disruptions. Partial reopening noted at Riyadh’s King Khalid International Airport. This is an active evacuation/ordered-departure measure tied to missile/drone threats.
**Estimated confidence:** High (direct official embassy alert and State Department order)

**Label:** Military Statement
**Time posted:** 13:18 UTC, 27 October 2026
**Source account:** U.S. Central Command (@CENTCOM)
**Link:** https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2115070611550581236
**Country:** United States (responding to Iran/IRGC claim)

**Estimated confidence:** High (direct official CENTCOM post)
