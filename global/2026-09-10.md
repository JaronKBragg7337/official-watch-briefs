## 3. IAEA Board of Governors refers Iran to the UN Security Council

- **Label:** Official Statement (Board action) / Independent Reporting of vote tally
- **Time posted / action date:** 9 September 2026
- **Source:** IAEA Board of Governors resolution submitted by the United Kingdom, France, Germany, and the United States; contemporaneous diplomatic reporting of the roll call
- **Country / body:** United Nations / IAEA (Iran nuclear file)
- **Summary:** The IAEA Board of Governors referred Iran to the UN Security Council by 23 votes in favor, 3 against (Russia, China, Niger), and 8 abstentions — the first such referral in 20 years over nuclear reporting non-compliance. The text notes grave concern at continued non-compliance, calls on Iran to remedy it, and asks the Director General to report to the Security Council and General Assembly. Russia, China, and Iran issued a joint statement calling the draft illegal and asserting continued cooperation under wartime conditions. No official Iranian announcement of NPT withdrawal, inspector expulsion, or resumed enrichment was issued through the 10 September cutoff. IAEA standing figures remain 440.9 kg UF6 up to 60% and 184.1 kg up to 20%; the Agency has said it cannot verify location/status of well over 200 kg of the 60% material believed to have survived at Isfahan and Natanz.
- **Estimated confidence:** High on the referral and vote outcome (Board action + multiple official/diplomatic confirmations); Moderate on current location of remaining HEU without restored IAEA access
