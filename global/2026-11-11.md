## 2. IRGC Spokesman Announces Lifting of Self-Imposed 2,000 km Ballistic Missile Range Limit
- **Label:** Military Statement / Government Claim (confirmed by Multiple Independent Reports)
- **Time posted:** 7 November 2026 (announcement); widely reported 7–10 November
- **Source:** IRGC Spokesman Brigadier General Hossein Mohebbi (via Iranian state-linked media including Tasnim, ISNA)
- **Link:** Multiple reports, e.g. https://www.timesofisrael.com/irgc-says-its-self-imposed-limit-on-ballistic-missile-range-has-been-lifted/ and Al Jazeera coverage
- **Country:** Iran
- **Summary:** IRGC spokesman Hossein Mohebbi stated at a press conference that Iran’s long-standing self-imposed 2,000-kilometer limit on ballistic missile range “has been lifted.” He indicated capabilities would be demonstrated in practice rather than through further statements. The announcement followed directives attributed to Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei authorizing responses without geographic limits. Iranian officials have also referenced underground missile production facilities. Independent reporting notes that Iran previously claimed the cap for defensive purposes and that longer-range attempts (e.g., toward Diego Garcia) have been reported in the past.
- **Estimated confidence:** High (multiple independent outlets citing the same official Iranian statements)

## 3. U.S. CENTCOM Establishes Task Force Iron Gate to Disrupt Iranian Ground Weapons Transfers
- **Label:** Official Military Statement
- **Time posted:** 7 November 2026
- **Source account / office:** U.S. Central Command (@CENTCOM) – official public release
- **Link:** https://www.centcom.mil/MEDIA/PUBLIC-RELEASES/Article/4644250/ (and corresponding X post)
- **Country:** United States
- **Summary:** CENTCOM Commander Adm. Brad Cooper directed the formation of Task Force Iron Gate, a roughly 20-person interagency team based in Tampa. The task force will coordinate efforts to monitor, detect, and disrupt the movement of advanced weapons systems, dual-use technologies, sanctioned materials, and illegal revenue streams across Iran’s land borders and the Caspian Sea. Officials described it as reinforcing the existing naval blockade that has already prevented over $25 billion in Iranian oil exports.
- **Estimated confidence:** High (direct official CENTCOM release)

- **Label:** Official Statement
- **Time posted:** 6–10 November 2026
- **Source:** U.S. Department of State / U.S. Embassies (including Jerusalem, Saudi Arabia, and others)
- **Link examples:** https://il.usembassy.gov/security-alert-u-s-embassy-jerusalem-november-6-2026/ and parallel mission alerts
