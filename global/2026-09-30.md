## 1. U.S. Treasury sanctions on Iran weapons-procurement networks

- **Label:** Official Statement
- **Time posted:** 2026-09-29 (Treasury press release dated September 29, 2026; widely reported same day)
- **Source account:** U.S. Department of the Treasury / OFAC (Operation Economic Outcast); statements attributed to Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent
- **Link:** https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0637
- **Country:** United States (targets in Iran, China, Hong Kong, Pakistan, Russia)
- **Summary:** OFAC designated individuals and entities it said procured weapons and dual-use components for Iran’s Ministry of Defense and Armed Forces Logistics (MODAFL), including a Beijing-based MODAFL representative, Iran-based Kavoshcom Asia R&D Group, Hong Kong-based EC Mojo Technology and its China-based representative, and additional nodes in Pakistan and Russia. Treasury said the action is intended to degrade reconstitution of Iran’s weapons, missile, and UAV programs and raise costs for enablers. Independent reporting described 10–13 designations in the same package.
- **Estimated confidence:** High
