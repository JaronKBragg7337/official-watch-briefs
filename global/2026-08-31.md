## 1. United States / CENTCOM: Strike on IRGC Minelaying Launchers on Larak Island; Rebuttal of “Act of Aggression” Claim

- **Label:** Military Statement
- **Time posted:** Monday, August 31, 2026 00:57:47 GMT (Aug 30, 8:57 PM EDT)
- **Source account:** @CENTCOM (U.S. Central Command)
- **Link:** https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2094228119079067852
- **Country:** United States
- **Summary:** CENTCOM rejected an IRGC claim that U.S. strikes preventing mine placement in the Strait of Hormuz were an “act of aggression,” calling the claim false. The command stated U.S. forces took “limited, precise action against IRGC minelaying forces posing an imminent threat in the Strait of Hormuz,” adding that Iran created the threat and the U.S. military eliminated it to protect civilian mariners, commercial shipping, and the free flow of global commerce. Independent reporting and CENTCOM spokesman Capt. Tim Hawkins separately identified the targets as two Iranian rocket launchers on Larak Island observed preparing to fire rockets carrying sea mines. This was the first publicly acknowledged U.S. strike on Iranian territory since late July after a roughly month-long pause in such strikes.
- **Estimated confidence:** High
