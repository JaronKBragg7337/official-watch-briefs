## 1. President Trump: Iran signs a deal or “won’t exist any longer”

- **Label:** Official Statement
- **Time posted:** 01 Oct 2026, afternoon (South Lawn remarks en route to Oklahoma, reported ~17:19 CDT / 22:19 UTC by The Hill; related Oval Office remarks the day prior)
- **Source account:** President Donald Trump, on-record remarks to reporters. Not posted as a standalone text on @WhiteHouse in this scan. Related reporting: Politico (01 Oct) on earlier “blow them up or make a deal” comments.
- **Link:** https://thehill.com/homenews/administration/6124187-trump-threatens-iran-peace-deal/
- **Country:** United States (subject: Iran)
- **Summary:** President Trump told reporters that he must decide whether Iran signs “a very fair deal” or “won’t exist any longer.” The Hill reported the South Lawn comments on 1 October. Politico reported that a day earlier, at an Oval Office briefing, he said the choice was to “blow them up or make a deal,” and that he had rejected Tehran’s latest proposal, which Iranian officials had framed around reopening the Strait of Hormuz in exchange for lifting the U.S. naval blockade of Iranian ports, waiving oil sanctions, and releasing frozen assets. Fox News reported he separately told Time that further bombing after the November midterms was “possible” and that the U.S. had been rebuilding munitions stocks. These are on-record presidential statements carried by multiple outlets, not a White House emergency alert, a strike order, or a DEFCON announcement.
- **Estimated confidence:** High that the remarks were made (multiple independent news organizations quoting the president); Moderate on any operational timeline, which was not specified in an official order
