**Link:** https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2116128769190859099  
**Country:** United States (operations involving Iran)  
**Summary:** CENTCOM Commander Admiral Brad Cooper released a video update stating that he visited forces in the Middle East, including over the Strait of Hormuz. He emphasized ongoing coordinated protection for commercial shipping and thanked service members.
