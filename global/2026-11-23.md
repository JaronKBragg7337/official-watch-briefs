### 1. Iran MFA: US Naval Blockade Constitutes “Military Aggression”; Iran Will Use All Means to Counter It
- **Label**: Official Statement
- **Time posted**: 23 November 2026 (weekly press briefing in Tehran; reported widely same day)
- **Source account**: Iranian Foreign Ministry Spokesperson Esmaeil Baghaei (via official MFA statements and IRNA)
- **Link**: https://www.en.mfa.ir (statements) and contemporaneous IRNA/English reporting
- **Country**: Iran
- **Summary**: Spokesperson Esmaeil Baghaei stated that the U.S. naval blockade of Iranian ports “undoubtedly constitutes an act of military aggression” under UN General Assembly Resolution 3314 and violates Article 2(4) of the UN Charter. He affirmed Iran’s right under Article 51 to use “all available means” to defend itself, noting that political, diplomatic, and defensive measures have already been decided at the highest levels. Baghaei added that security in the Strait of Hormuz can be restored “in the shortest possible time” if the United States lifts sanctions and the blockade and ends hostilities on all fronts (including Lebanon). He also said Iran has made no decision on NPT withdrawal and protested a Qatar-Germany joint statement.
- **Estimated confidence**: High (direct official spokesperson remarks)
