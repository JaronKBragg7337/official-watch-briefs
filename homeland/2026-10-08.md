## Alert 1 — Hurricane Isaias: storm-surge and hurricane warnings; local evacuation orders

- **Time:** National Hurricane Center Advisory Number 8, 10:00 a.m. CDT / 11:00 a.m. EDT, Thursday, October 8, 2026. Local evacuation notices reported the same morning.
- **Official source:** NOAA / National Weather Service National Hurricane Center (Miami). Supporting local emergency management: Okaloosa County, Florida; Alabama coastal guidance and a state emergency declaration by Governor Kay Ivey, as reported by news outlets citing county and state releases. FEMA’s same-day newsroom items were routine major-disaster declarations for earlier events (Guam, New Hampshire, Wisconsin), not this storm.
- **Link:** https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/text/MIATCPAT4.shtml
- **Summary:** At 10:00 a.m. CDT, NHC reported Hurricane Isaias near 23.7N 90.2W, about 380 miles south of the mouth of the Mississippi River, with maximum sustained winds of 85 mph and movement east-northeast at about 10 mph. A Storm Surge Warning was in effect from the mouth of the Mississippi River to the Suwannee River. A Hurricane Warning covered Ocean Springs, Mississippi, to the Bay/Gulf County line in Florida. Tropical storm warnings flanked that zone. NHC said preparations to protect life and property from storm surge and damaging winds in the warning area should be rushed to completion today, and that people in surge-warning areas should promptly follow evacuation and other instructions from local officials. Landfall was forecast along the northern Gulf Coast late Friday or early Saturday. Separately, Okaloosa County, Florida, ordered mandatory evacuations beginning noon Thursday for Okaloosa Island and portions of coastal Fort Walton Beach, Destin, Mary Esther, and Niceville. Alabama issued voluntary evacuation guidance for coastal and low-lying areas, including encouragement to leave Gulf Shores and Orange Beach, and the governor declared a state of emergency for 40 counties.
- **Area affected:** Northern Gulf Coast — coastal Louisiana (mouth of the Mississippi eastward), coastal Mississippi, coastal Alabama (including Mobile Bay), and the Florida Panhandle through the Suwannee River area for surge, with the hurricane warning from Ocean Springs, MS, to the Bay/Gulf County line, FL.
- **Immediate public action recommended:** Yes, for people inside the official warning and local evacuation zones. Follow local emergency-management evacuation orders, complete surge and wind preparations today, and do not rely on this brief in place of hurricanes.gov or local officials.
- **Confidence:** Official Statement (NHC advisory). Local evacuation orders: Official + Independent Reporting (county and state actions reported by Newsweek and other outlets citing official releases; not re-verified on each county site in this pass).
