## Alert: Recommended Relocation / Evacuation Support for Controlled Detonation at Iowa Army Ammunition Plant

**Time:** Advisory issued November 24, 2026. Detonation planned for approximately 9:00 a.m. CST on Wednesday, November 25, 2026 (precise time subject to weather conditions). Recommended relocation commencing November 24, 2026.

**Official source:** U.S. Army (Army Sustainment Command), with interagency support from FEMA, EPA, CDC, and other federal partners.

**Link to the original statement:** https://www.army.mil/article/297039/public_safety_advisory_controlled_mitigated_detonation_of_unstable_lead_azide_at_iowa_army_ammunition_plant

**One paragraph summary:** The U.S. Army is conducting a controlled, mitigated detonation of unstable, degraded lead azide (approximately 68 barrels / ~5 tons) stored at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant in Middletown, Iowa, to eliminate the risk of a spontaneous, uncontrolled explosion. The material has crystallized over decades of storage and is highly sensitive to friction, shock, and temperature changes; safer alternatives such as chemical treatment or relocation were determined too dangerous by multi-agency experts. Extensive mitigation measures including physical barriers, aqueous foam, water saturation, and atmospheric modeling are in place. FEMA is supporting a recommended relocation (evacuation zone) for residents of Middletown and a 5-mile cone southwest of the plant, providing transportation, temporary shelter/lodging (through at least November 27), food, and supplies. Residents outside this zone are advised to shelter in place. Post-event sampling of air, soil, and water will be conducted and results published.

**What area is affected:** Middletown, Iowa, and the defined 5-mile cone extending southwest of the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant (IAAAP), based on Defense Threat Reduction Agency dispersion and deposition modeling (maps available and updated on Army sites). Surrounding areas may experience potential lead dust deposition; temporary flight restrictions may be in effect in the vicinity.

**Whether immediate public action is recommended:** Yes. Individuals in the recommended relocation zone should contact FEMA at 1-800-621-3362 or register via DisasterAssistance.gov for assistance and relocate out of an abundance of caution. Those outside the zone should shelter in place (close windows/doors, set HVAC to recirculate, minimize outdoor air intake, protect infants/children under 6, pregnant individuals, and those with chronic health conditions), avoid unnecessary travel through affected areas, and monitor official Army updates and maps. After the all-clear, follow Iowa HHS and EPA guidance for surface cleaning regarding potential lead exposure.

**Confidence level:** Official Statement (primary source is the U.S. Army public safety advisory on army.mil; supported by FEMA involvement and prior interagency statements from EPA and others).
