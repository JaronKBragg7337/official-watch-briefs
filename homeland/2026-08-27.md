### Summary

President Trump declared a national emergency under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act and the National Emergencies Act over an “unusual and extraordinary foreign threat” from bulk-power system equipment produced abroad. The stated concern is that foreign actors can create or exploit vulnerabilities in equipment that supports national defense, emergency services, critical infrastructure, and the economy (sabotage, unauthorized access, disruption).

The order generally prohibits or conditions purchase and installation of certain foreign-produced bulk-power equipment and associated software, firmware, and remote-access capabilities. The Secretary of Energy may impose conditions on existing equipment, including identify, isolate, monitor, secure, disconnect, replace, or remove items judged an unacceptable risk. Covered hardware includes transformers, generators, turbines, battery storage, inverters, high-voltage breakers, protective relays, and industrial control systems.

The fact sheet explicitly ties higher stakes to “the rapid growth of advanced manufacturing, artificial intelligence, and defense production” increasing U.S. dependence on reliable electricity and on some foreign components. The order text does **not** name China. It does **not** mention Bitcoin, blockchain, or crypto mining.

This is a legal/economic national-emergency declaration about supply-chain and cyber risk in high-voltage grid gear. It is not a civil-defense alert that the grid is under attack today.
