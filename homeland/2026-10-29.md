# Homeland Security Brief - October 29, 2026

## Alert: Nuclear Facility Incident at Savannah River Site

- **Time:** Site Area Emergency declared approximately 5:07–5:39 p.m. EDT on October 28, 2026; terminated at 9:45 p.m. EDT on October 28, 2026.
- **Official Source:** Savannah River Site Emergency Response Organization (U.S. Department of Energy)
- **Link:** https://public.srs.gov/2026/10/savannah-river-site-emergency-response-event-news-release-3/
- **Summary:** Officials at the U.S. Department of Energy’s Savannah River Site near Aiken, South Carolina, declared a Site Area Emergency following a fire at the Savannah River National Laboratory. The fire was extinguished, radiological surveys found no detectable levels of contamination outside the affected facility, and one employee sustained minor injuries. Temporary restrictions were placed on site access, rail, river, and air traffic in the vicinity; some employees sheltered in place as a precaution. The emergency was terminated later that evening, protective actions were lifted, and normal operations resumed.
- **Area Affected:** Savannah River Site and immediate vicinity (near Aiken, SC); temporary flight restriction approximately 15 nautical miles radius.
- **Immediate Public Action Recommended:** During the event, local residents were advised to monitor local radio and TV for any instructions from state officials. As of the termination, no ongoing public action is recommended; protective measures have been lifted.
- **Confidence Level:** Official Statement + Independent Reporting

No other significant homeland security developments matching the specified criteria were detected in official communications as of 11:25 a.m. EDT on October 29, 2026. Routine activities such as VIP Temporary Flight Restrictions enforced by NORAD and the annual Global Thunder nuclear command-and-control exercise continue but do not constitute alerts under the monitoring criteria.
