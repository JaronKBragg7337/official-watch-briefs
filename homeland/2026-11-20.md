### Notes on Recent Context (for awareness, not current alerts)

Related statements characterized the need for ATC modernization funding as a national emergency. No ongoing public action required.
- On November 17, 2026, FEMA and the FCC completed a routine nationwide test of the Emergency Alert System. No action was required by the public.

Such reporting does not constitute an official warning requiring public action.
