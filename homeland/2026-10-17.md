**Additional Context:** On October 16, 2026, the Department of Homeland Security, Department of Transportation (FAA), and Department of War jointly announced a temporary flight restriction (TFR) prohibiting unauthorized unmanned aircraft systems (drones) operations within one nautical mile of the U.S.-Mexico border, from the surface to 400 feet above ground level. The airspace is classified as national defense airspace. The measure aims to counter potential cartel drone activity, reduce risks to border security operations, and protect federal personnel and facilities. Drone operators must comply with NOTAMs and request waivers through CBP if needed; manned aircraft operations are unaffected. No immediate public action is recommended for the general population. Ongoing recovery efforts from Hurricane Isaias continue under previously issued FEMA emergency declarations for affected states (including Florida, Georgia, and Alabama). Routine monitoring, training, and operational activities continue across the monitored agencies, including USNORTHCOM homeland defense support and CENTCOM regional operations.
