## Notes on Recent Context

While no new alerts matching the criteria were identified today, the following recent official activity is noted for completeness:

- On November 13, 2026, the FAA issued a nationwide ground stop due to a telecommunications outage affecting air traffic control radar and communications systems. The ground stop was lifted the same evening (approximately 8:15 p.m. ET). Officials from DHS (Secretary Markwayne Mullin), DOT (Secretary Sean Duffy), and FAA stated there was zero evidence of a cyberattack or nefarious activity. The incident was attributed to overloaded legacy telecommunications equipment (data flooding from sites in Amarillo, TX and El Dorado, AR). The President declared a national emergency related to the outdated air traffic control infrastructure to facilitate congressional funding for modernization. All aircraft landed safely. Current status: Airspace is open; operations returning to normal with residual delays possible. Official sources: [FAA Q&A](https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/nationwide-ground-stop-questions-and-answers) (Nov 15), [DHS press release](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/11/13/icymi-secretary-mullin-and-secretary-duffy-address-nationwide-faa-ground-stop-all) (Nov 13).

- White House has assembled a "Transportation Technology Transformation Tiger Team" (comprising officials from FAA, Departments of Transportation and Defense, and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence) that has been meeting daily to prevent recurrence of air traffic control outages ahead of the Thanksgiving holiday travel period. Official White House release, November 23, 2026: [link](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/11/icymi-white-house-convenes-elite-team-to-prevent-air-traffic-outages-ahead-of-thanksgiving/).

- FBI, DHS, and TSA issued an industry-facing advisory to airlines, cargo carriers, and airports regarding terrorist group messaging encouraging attacks similar to a September attempted hijacking. Officials (including DHS) confirmed there are no known specific or credible threats to U.S. aviation at this time. This appears to be a routine precautionary notice.
